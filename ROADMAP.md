# Roadmap

## 0.1 alpha

- Agreement documents with sections and clauses, reorderable and multi-line.
- Proposals: a peer's node with no local counterpart is accepted or withdrawn
  rather than merged and reconciled afterwards.
- Consent-based organizational trees made from independently shared parent
  and subagreements with topic-scoped membership.
- Repeatable two-client direct and mailbox setup.
- Versioned application facade for cross-application consumers.

## Not yet

- Signing or tamper-evidence. An adopted agreement records what peers saw and
  accepted; it is not evidence against a peer who controls their own copy.
- Versioned releases of a document, distinct from the node history underneath.
- Organization-wide roles and delegated authority. These need their own
  agreement semantics; tree position alone must never imply authority.

Executable distribution follows only after the focused LGPL packaging review.
